## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Detailed explanation of the proposed solution:
Develop an end-to-end digital B2B2C marketplace platform (web and mobile application) named "FarmDirect AI" that bridges the gap between farmers/Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) and bulk buyers/retail consumers. The platform eliminates middlemen, enables direct price negotiation, secure digital escrow payments, and integrated logistics tracking.

How it addresses the problem:
By bypassing traditional multi-layered supply chain intermediaries (commission agents, multiple wholesalers), farmers receive a higher share of the consumer rupee (fair pricing), while consumers buy fresh produce at lower, transparent market rates.

Innovation and uniqueness of the solution:

Predictive Demand Forecasting: Uses Machine Learning models on historical consumption, weather, and festival data to predict crop demand, minimizing wastage.

Dynamic Route Optimization: An AI algorithm coordinates local transport schedules to aggregate loads from multiple farmers, reducing transit time and logistics costs.

Quality Grading Automation: Smartphone-based image processing to auto-grade agricultural produce for transparent pricing.

## Feasibility and Viability
Analysis of the feasibility of the idea:
High technical and economic feasibility. With government backing of FPOs and high smartphone/internet penetration in rural areas, onboarding farmers is increasingly practical. The use of open-source routing libraries and standard web frameworks keeps initial infrastructure costs low.

Potential challenges and risks:

Low digital literacy among smallholder farmers.

Resistance from traditional intermediaries or local mandi brokers.

Perishable nature of goods leading to supply chain bottlenecks.

Strategies for overcoming these challenges:

Collaborate closely with local FPO leaders and agricultural extension workers to conduct hands-on training camps.

Implement voice-assisted, multi-lingual vernacular UI options for ease of use.

Partner with local cold-storage providers and third-party logistics (3PL) fleets for reliable cold-chain transport.
## Impact and Benefits
Potential impact on the target audience:

Farmers & FPOs: 20–30% increase in profit margins due to direct market access and reduced commission cuts.

Consumers & Bulk Buyers: 10–15% reduction in grocery expenses and assured quality.

Benefits of the solution:

Economic: Boosts rural income and generates tech-enabled logistics employment.

Social: Empowers FPOs with better market intelligence and digital financial inclusion.

Environmental: Minimizes post-harvest food waste through optimized routing and demand-supply matching.
